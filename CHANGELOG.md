- 10 agents: book-planner (通用动态章节), 5 writers (foundation/core-loop/core-system/tools/integration), book-chapter-reviewer (逐章结构审查), book-technical-reviewer (技术事实核对), book-verifier, book-editor-in-chief
- `/source-code-book:start` command for full pipeline launch
- Complete documentation: README.md, workflow-experience.md
